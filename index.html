Questions

Common questions.

What file format do I get?
  • Standard MP4 files
  • Play on phones, laptops, smart TVs, tablets
  • No special software needed
How long does it take?
  • 2 to 3 weeks on average
  • Tapes play in real time, so a six hour tape takes six hours
  • Large collections take longer, I'll tell you up front
What if my tape is old or damaged?
  • Old tapes are the norm and most play fine
  • Mold, warping or snapped tape, I'll tell you before doing anything
  • No charge for a tape that can't be recovered
Will the video look better than the original?
  • No, and be wary of anyone who claims otherwise
  • Digitizing preserves what's there, it can't add detail never recorded
  • It does stop further decay, and tapes degrade every year
Do you take VHS-C camcorder tapes?
  • Yes, the smaller camcorder cassettes
  • Same price as full size VHS
My tapes were recorded overseas. Is that a problem?
  • Mention it up front, it matters
  • Overseas tapes are usually PAL or SECAM and need a different machine
  • The cassettes look identical, so I can't tell by looking
Can you copy store-bought movies or music?
  • No, copyrighted tapes, DVDs and CDs can't be duplicated
  • Home movies, home recordings and discs you made yourself only
How do I know my tapes won't get lost?
  • We write down every tape together and you keep a copy
  • They go to one place and come straight back
  • Never boxed up or handed to a courier
What happens to my tapes afterward?
  • You get every one of them back
  • Nothing is thrown away, kept or shipped anywhere
