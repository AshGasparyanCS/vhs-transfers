Questions

The ones that come up most.

What file format do I get?

Standard MP4 files, which play on essentially anything: phones, laptops, smart TVs, tablets. Nothing proprietary and no special software required.

How long does it take?

Tapes have to play in real time to be captured, so a six hour tape takes six hours of machine time. Most orders are ready within a week. Large collections may take a bit longer and I'll tell you up front.

What if my tape is old or damaged?

Old tapes are the norm and most play fine. If a tape is moldy, badly warped, or the tape itself is snapped, I'll let you know before doing anything and you won't be charged for a tape that can't be recovered.

Will the video look better than the original?

Honestly, no, and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. VHS is a low-resolution format and digitizing preserves what's on the tape, it doesn't add detail that was never recorded. What it does do is stop any further decay, since tapes genuinely degrade every year they sit in a closet.

Do you take VHS-C camcorder tapes?

Yes. Those are the smaller camcorder cassettes and they're handled at the same price as full size VHS. Just bring them along.

Can you copy store-bought movies?

No. Commercially produced and copyrighted tapes can't be duplicated. This service is for home movies and personal recordings.

Do I really not have to drop anything off?

Correct. I pick the tapes up from your home and return them the same way, at no extra charge within the local area. If you're further out, just ask and we'll sort something out.

How do I know my tapes won't get lost?

When I collect them, we write down every tape together and you keep a copy of that list. They go straight to one place, get digitized, and come back to you. They're never boxed up and handed to a courier.

What happens to my tapes afterward?

You get every one of them back. Nothing is thrown away, kept, or shipped anywhere.
